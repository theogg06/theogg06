# Research kit

Everything needed to run discovery interviews (Phase 0) and usability sessions ([`testing/`](testing/), Phase 4).

> **Nothing in this folder is a research finding.** Personas and JTBD are hypotheses drawn from desk research. Results tables are blank on purpose. Real findings go in `notes/` and `synthesis/` (create these folders when you start, and keep raw notes out of the public repo if they're at all identifiable).

| # | File | What it is |
|---|---|---|

## Research plan (summary)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Understand the decisions analysts, finance managers and compliance officers make with economic data, and how missing, suspect or inaccessible data affects them |
| **Method** | Semi-structured critical-incident interviews |
| **Participants** | 4–5, at least 1 AT user; see `03-recruitment.md` |
| **Timeline (suggested)** | Week 1 recruit · Week 2–3 interviews · Week 3 synthesis · Week 4 revise personas/JTBD, then usability testing on the prototype |
| **Output** | Affinity map → insights → revised personas and JTBD → design changes logged in `../DECISIONS.md` |

## After the interviews

Upload or paste your notes (anonymised, one file per participant), and I'll help with:
1. atomising them into the affinity CSV,
2. clustering themes with participant counts,
3. marking each persona row and JTBD as ✅ / ✏️ / ❌ with evidence,
4. turning insights into design changes.
