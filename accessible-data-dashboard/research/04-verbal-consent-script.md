# Verbal consent script

**Use when:** a participant uses a screen reader, prefers not to sign a document, or asks to go through consent out loud. You can also use it for anyone at the start of a remote session.

**Before the session:** Email the consent information (`04-consent-form.md` as plain text in the email body, not only as an attachment) at least 48 hours in advance, so the participant can read it with their own tools at their own pace.

**Recording:** Start the recording **only after** they agree to item 4 below. Then repeat items 1–7 briefly *on* the recording so there's a record of consent. If they decline recording, write each answer down, with the time, in the note template and email them a summary of their answers afterwards.

**Pace:** Read one item at a time. Pause after each one and wait for a clear "yes" or "no". If the answer is unclear, ask again in other words. Never treat silence as consent.

---

> Before we start, I'd like to go through the consent information together. It takes about three minutes. I'll read one short section at a time, and you can stop me or ask a question whenever you like.
>
> **About the study.** I'm researching how people make decisions with financial and economic data, and what goes wrong when the data or the tools let them down. What I learn will help design an accessible data dashboard, which is an open, non-commercial project.
>
> **What happens.** We'll talk for about 45 minutes, and longer if you'd like. You can skip any question, take a break, or stop at any time, and you don't need to give a reason. Please don't share anything confidential about your employer or clients.
>
> **Your information.** I'll use a code instead of your name in my notes. If I quote you in published work, I'll remove anything that could identify you, such as your name, your employer, or specific details. Your contact details are kept separately and deleted within 30 days of the study ending.
>
> **Recording.** I'd like to record the session so my notes are accurate. Only I'll use it, and I'll delete it within 30 days. You can say no and still take part.
>
> **Your rights.** You can withdraw at any time, even after today, by emailing [your email]. I'll then delete your notes and recording. You can also ask to see or correct what I hold. The full details are in the email I sent you on [date].
>
> **Thank you payment.** [Amount, and when it'll arrive. Say you'll get it even if you stop early.]
>
> Do you have any questions so far?

*[Answer the questions. Then:]*

> I'll now ask a few yes-or-no questions.
>
> 1. Have you had a chance to hear or read the information and ask questions?
> 2. Do you understand that taking part is voluntary, and that you can withdraw at any time?
> 3. Do you agree to take part in this interview?
> 4. Do you agree to the session being recorded?
>
> *[If yes to 4: "Thank you. I'm starting the recording now." Start recording, then:]* "The recording has started. Can you confirm again that you agree to be recorded?"
>
> 5. Do you agree that anonymised quotes from this conversation may be used in published work?
> 6. If you choose to tell me about any assistive technology or accessibility settings you use, do you agree that I can note that in anonymised form?
> 7. Optional: would you like to be contacted about trying out a prototype later?
>
> Thank you. Your participant code is **P__**. Use it if you ever want to withdraw. I'll email you a copy of these answers today.
