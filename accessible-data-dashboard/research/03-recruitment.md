# Recruitment: screener and outreach

## Target mix (4–5 interviews)

| Slot | Role | Must have | Nice to have |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Analyst (economic, financial, risk or data) | Uses time-series or financial data at least weekly | Builds reports for others |
| 1 | Finance manager / head of a finance function | Makes or approves decisions based on reports at least monthly | Presents to a board or executive team |
| 1 | Compliance, risk or regulatory reporting officer | Checks or signs off figures that others report | Experience with supervisory reporting |
| ≥1 across the above | Assistive technology user | Uses a screen reader, screen magnifier, voice control, or high-contrast or zoom settings for work | — |

Aim for variety in organisation type (bank, fintech, corporate finance, public sector) and seniority. **Don't recruit from your own team or anyone you manage.** Power dynamics distort answers.

---

## Screener (5 minutes, online form or email)

Use an accessible form tool, such as Microsoft Forms or Google Forms with default themes, and test it with a screen reader before sending it out. Questions marked **[Q]** qualify or disqualify. The rest help balance the sample.

1. **[Q]** Which best describes your current role?
   - Analyst (economic, financial, risk or data)
   - Finance manager or head of finance
   - Compliance, risk or regulatory reporting
   - Other (please describe) → *qualify only if the description involves data-driven decisions*
2. **[Q]** How often do you use data reports or dashboards to make or support decisions at work?
   - Daily · Weekly · Monthly → **qualify**
   - Less than monthly · Never → **thank and close**
3. Which kinds of data do you work with? (select all)
   - Macroeconomic (inflation, rates, unemployment) · Market or FX · Credit or lending · Internal financial (budget, P&L) · Regulatory or supervisory · Other
4. Which tools do you use? (select all)
   - Excel / spreadsheets · Power BI · Tableau · Bloomberg or Refinitiv · In-house dashboards · Python or R · Other
5. Type of organisation: Bank · Fintech · Insurance · Corporate · Public sector or central bank · Consultancy · Other
6. **Optional:** Do you use any assistive technology or accessibility settings when working on a computer? (select all, or skip)
   - Screen reader (e.g. NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, Narrator)
   - Screen magnification (e.g. ZoomText, built-in magnifier)
   - Browser zoom or large text
   - High contrast or custom colours
   - Voice control or dictation
   - Alternative keyboard, switch or other input
   - Other (please describe)
   - None
   - Prefer not to say
   > *Why we ask:* We want our research to include people who use these tools, because dashboards often don't work well for them. Answering is optional, and it won't affect whether you're invited unless you'd like to be included for this reason.
7. Is there anything that would help you take part (e.g. captions, extra time, questions in advance, a particular meeting platform)? *(free text, optional)*
8. **[Q]** Are you available for a 45-minute video or phone call between [dates]? Yes / No
9. Email for scheduling. *(This is stored separately from research notes and deleted after the study; see the consent form.)*

**Disqualify:** anyone who works in UX research or on dashboard products at a direct competitor, and anyone in your own reporting line.

## Outreach message: assistive technology users (accessibility communities)

**Where to post (follow each community's rules and ask moderators first):**
- National blind and partially sighted associations' research-participation lists (e.g. in your country of residence)
- Employee disability networks in financial institutions (ask the network lead before posting)
- Online accessibility communities such as the WebAIM discussion list and accessibility-focused LinkedIn groups, plus local A11y meetups
- Paid AT-user panels (e.g. Fable, Access Works by Knowbility), if you have budget. These handle compensation and consent logistics.

> **Subject: Paid research: how do you work with charts and financial data using assistive technology?**
>
> Hello,
>
> I'm [Your name], a designer working on an open-source data dashboard for finance professionals. I want it to work well with screen readers, magnification, high contrast, voice control and keyboard-only use, and I'd like to learn from people who use these tools every day.
>
> **Who I'm looking for:** people who use assistive technology or accessibility settings at work, and who deal with numbers, reports or data. You don't need a finance job title. Accounting, admin, analysis, research or public-sector work all count.
>
> **What's involved:** a 45–60 minute conversation by phone or video, on the platform of your choice. I'll ask about how you currently deal with charts, tables and dashboards, and what gets in the way. There's no test, and you won't need to use anything new.
>
> **Your time:** [€X voucher or payment], including any extra time the session takes.
>
> **Access:** I can send the questions in advance, use captions, take breaks, or run the session by phone. Tell me what works for you.
>
> **Privacy:** Your answers are anonymised. I won't ask about medical details. You can withdraw at any time.
>
> To take part, reply to this message or fill in this short form: [link]. Tested with NVDA and VoiceOver; email works too.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name] · [contact email]

**Notes for the AT recruit:**
- **Pay AT users at least the same rate, and pay for the extra time.** Sessions often run longer because of AT setup, not because of the participant.
- Don't call people "inspirational" or describe the research as "helping" them. They're experts consulted for their expertise.
- Offer an alternative to the form (email or phone). Test the form with a screen reader before posting.
