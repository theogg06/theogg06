## What this study is about

I'm a designer researching how analysts, finance managers and compliance professionals use data to make decisions. I'm also researching what goes wrong when data is missing or hard to access. What I learn will shape the design of an accessible data dashboard, which I'm building as an open, non-commercial portfolio project.

## What you'll be asked to do

- Talk with me for about **45 minutes** (up to 60 if you'd like more time), by video, phone or in person.
- I'll ask about your work, a recent decision you made using data, and how you deal with problems in data or tools.
- You can skip any question without giving a reason.

**You do not need to share anything confidential about your employer or clients.** Please don't name clients, share confidential figures, or show internal systems on screen. If you mention something confidential by accident, tell me and I'll delete it.

## Recording

With your permission, I'll make an **audio or video recording** so I can take accurate notes. If you'd prefer, the session can be notes-only.

- Recordings are used **only by me** to write notes.
- I'll **delete recordings within 30 days** of the session, once my notes are complete.
- Recordings will **not** be published or shared.

## How your information is used and stored

- I'll replace your name with a **participant code** (for example, "P03") in all notes.
- I may quote things you said in my published case study or portfolio, but **without your name, employer or any detail that could identify you.** I'll remove or generalise identifying details (for example, "a large bank" instead of the bank's name).
- Your contact details are kept **separately** from your interview notes and deleted within **30 days of the study ending**.
- Anonymised notes are stored in [storage location, e.g. "an encrypted folder on my personal laptop and an EU-hosted cloud drive with two-factor authentication"] and kept for up to **[24 months]**, then deleted.
- I won't sell or share your information with anyone else.
- If you tell me about assistive technology or accessibility settings you use, I'll record only what's relevant to the research, such as "uses a screen reader". I won't ask about medical conditions.

## Your rights (GDPR)

This study is covered by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). **I am the data controller.** The legal basis for processing your data is **your consent** (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). For any information about disability or assistive technology you choose to share, the basis is your **explicit consent** (Article 9(2)(a)).

You have the right to:

- **Withdraw at any time**, before, during or after the session, without giving a reason and without any consequence. If you withdraw, I'll delete your recording and notes. Anything already published in anonymised form can't be withdrawn, because it can no longer be linked to you.
- **Access** the information I hold about you, and **ask me to correct or delete** it.
- **Complain** to your national data protection authority (in Romania, the ANSPDCP; elsewhere in the EU, your local authority).

To withdraw or use any of these rights, email **[your email]** and quote your participant code if you have it.
