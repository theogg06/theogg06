# Consent and compensation notes for usability sessions

## Consent
- Reuse `../04-consent-form.md` / `.docx` and `../04-verbal-consent-script.md`. Change the purpose line to: "try out an early prototype of a data dashboard and think aloud while using it".
- Add one item for usability sessions: **"I agree that my screen (and, if I choose, system audio) is shared and recorded during the session."** Recording the screen can capture other windows and notifications. Ask participants to close unrelated apps, and offer a fresh browser profile.
- Screen reader users: the verbal script is the default. Email the text 48 hours in advance.
- Remind participants that the prototype contains **synthetic data** in some tasks, and that nothing they see is financial advice.

## Compensation
| | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Amount | Same hourly rate for everyone: [€X per 60 min] |
| AT sessions | Pay for **the extra 15 min** as well (75 min). Setup and audio sharing take time because of the tools, not the person |
| Form | Voucher or bank transfer, the participant's choice. Accessible voucher platforms only. Test the redemption flow with a screen reader first |
| When | Within [5] working days, **even if the session ends early or the participant withdraws** |
| Records | Payment records are kept separately from research data, under financial-record retention rules |

## Withdrawal
If a participant withdraws, delete their recording, notes and observation sheet. Remove their rows from any findings tables, and note "1 participant withdrew" in the report. Data that's already anonymised and published can't be removed; the consent form says so.
